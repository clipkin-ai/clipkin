# Production map

Come here with a concrete question and jump straight to the page that owns it. The groups below are
a directory, not a pipeline: most revisions touch one or two pages, and nothing requires walking
through every row. [System relationships](system.md) is the shared mental model behind all of them.
The creative decisions themselves come from Creation and from the relevant
[Format or Craft](../playbooks/index.md).

Two common shapes of work:

- **Performance-led pieces** (straight A-roll, short drama, a talking presenter). Write the intended
  passages in Script. The accepted, normalized take tells you how long each passage really is; give
  that Extent a place on Timeline, then route its picture and sound explicitly through Visual and
  Audio Tracks. Bring in NarrativeAlignment only when something must know where words fall inside
  the take: Caption, semantically timed graphics or another consumer. Naming meaning in Script still
  pays off when no consumer needs word timing and no motion graphics are planned.
- **A standalone asset edit** (crop this image, cut that clip). Perform the actual media operation
  and hand over its Output; no film assembly is involved.
